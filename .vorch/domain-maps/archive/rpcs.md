# Archive: RPCs

The archive RPCs of `server/rpc/archive_methods.py` and the archive fields of the deleting RPCs, as accessors see them: parameters, results, error codes and their `data`. Archive semantics: `archive.md`.

`archive_methods.py` serves the archive (`archive.md`): `archive.list` (filters `kind`, `agent_id` (an `agent@project` address also sets the Project), `project_id`, JSON `cursor`, `limit` 1-200, default 50) returns `{entries, next_cursor, retention_days, retention_unknown}` (`retention_unknown: true`: vBot cannot read the period, deletes nothing for retention, and `retention_days` and every `purge_at` are `null`), each entry with `origin`, `purge_at`, `restorable`, `not_restorable_reason`, `may_hold_user_folders`, `accepts_target_id` (a restore may name a `target_id`), `session_count` (every member) and `review_fork_count` (the members that are review forks of another member); `archive.show` (`session_limit` 1-500, default 100) adds the member Sessions, each with `review_fork`, the payload trees with `user_folder` marks, the recorded details (an external Workspace's `details.workspace.path` is absolute) and the full restore check (`restore.possible`, `target_id`, `blockers`, `warnings`); `archive.restore {entry_id, target_id?}` runs under `_guard_agent_lifecycle`, returns the restored id or Session address, `grant_agent_ids` and the `warnings` (each problem with its facts, like `archive.show`), and publishes the agents, projects or scoped sessions invalidation of what it restored; `archive.purge` takes exactly one of `entry_ids` (1-100) or `all: true` with the list filters and returns `{purged, pending, skipped, gone, kept}`; a pending entry (`{entry_id, reason}`: `usage_import_failed`, `stopped` or the failure's exception class) began its deletion, the retention sweep continues it, and a later purge continues it at once (`archive.md` -> Retention); a skipped entry (`{entry_id, reason, state}`, reason `busy`, `usage_import_failed` or a failure's class) was not deleted and stays as it is; `gone` lists the ids another operation deleted or restored first; `kept` (`{entry_id, reason}`, reason `files` or `user_folders`) lists the matching entries `all: true` never deletes because they may hold the user's folders. Expected archive errors map to `archive_entry_not_found` (`data.entry_ids`), `archive_entry_busy` (`data.entry_id`, `state`; from a restore check or a named purge also `message`, what that state means for the caller, and for an interrupted restore that cannot finish `path` and `problem`), `archive_restore_conflict` (`data.entry_id`, `kind`, `conflicts`, `fix: "target_id"`: retry with another `target_id`) and `archive_not_restorable` (`data.entry_id`, `blockers`); each problem carries `code`, `message` and its facts. `agent.delete`, `project.rm` and `session.delete` accept `permanent: true`: they archive as before, then purge the entry they created after releasing the reference lock (`purge_permanently`), and every delete result carries `archive_entry_id` (always the entry's id), `purged`, `purge_pending` and `purge_reason` (`null` once purged or without `permanent`; the pending reason, `busy`, `gone`, or a failure's class otherwise; a failing permanent purge never fails the delete); `agent.delete` also returns `session_count` and `external_workspace` (the absolute path of a Workspace outside the Agent's own directory, left in place), `project.rm` `session_count`. The CLI reads these deletes, `archive.restore` and `archive.purge` without a response deadline (`cli.md`). Tests: `tests/server/rpc/test_archive_methods.py`.
