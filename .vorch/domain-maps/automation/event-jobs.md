# Event Jobs

Read when changing Cron jobs of schedule type `event`, which run at the occurrences of a calendar event: event times, owed occurrences and their windows, firing and progress, the event context note, event-change checks and deletion with the event. The calendar side is in `../calendar.md`; schedules, catch-up and claims shared with the other Cron jobs in `cron.md`.

`event_id`, `event_edge` (`start`|`end`) and `event_offset_minutes` (signed, within 31 days) bind a job to every occurrence of a calendar event. RPC, CLI and Tool spell edge and offset as an event time such as `start - 30m` (`CronService.parse_event_time` / `parse_event_schedule`; `format_schedule` gives the canonical `start`, `end + 2h`, `start - 1d`); a job switched to `event` without one runs at `start`. Create, a schedule change and enabling require the event to exist ("Calendar event not found: <id>"; an unreadable calendar refuses with `CronStorageError`). The job's task (`_run_event_job`) fires once per occurrence at its due time; moved occurrences come due at their new time, removed ones never, and every calendar change (also `set_timezone`) wakes it. An occurrence is owed when it came due after the job's coverage (the later of `created_at` and `covered_until`), or at that boundary with an unconsumed id in matching `event_progress`, and its window is still open, after the practice of event-bound reminders (Outlook dismisses reminders of past events, push notifications expire at the event deadline): due before the occurrence's start, until the start; due during it, until its end; due at or after its end, until the event's next occurrence starts, and without limit after the last one. Each owed occurrence starts its own Run, earliest first. Firing persists `covered_until` and additive `event_progress` (its own `due_at` and consumed `occurrence_ids`) before the trigger, so tied occurrences each run once, including after restart, and an attempted occurrence is used even when its Run fails; `last_attempt_at` is no part of event coverage. Missing progress, or progress whose timestamp no longer matches coverage, leaves the boundary fully covered as in earlier files. After obtaining a Run slot and again after saving its claim, the job re-reads its occurrence and catch-up window; an invalidated claim restores its previous progress before rescheduling, a failed claim save starts no Run and backs off, and context and lateness reflect admission time. Activation and schedule changes set `covered_until` to now and clear progress as for other jobs, so occurrences due before are not owed; event edits do not, so an occurrence moved later comes due again and one moved into an open window starts late. The Run's message is the prompt only; `context_note` carries the event (`_cron_events.context_note`): `Cron job <id> is due (<event time>) for the calendar event "<title>" (event <id>[, occurrence <id>]).`, `Event time: <start> to <end> (<zone>)` (all-day: `<day>, all day` or `<first> to <last day>, all day`), optional `Location:` and `Description:` lines, then for a late start a blank line and the late notice. An event job never completes: with no occurrence owed or ahead, or without its event, it stays active and waits for a calendar change; `next_fire_at` is then None and `can_fire` false, so it is no live reference (`../automation.md` -> Automation references). Deleting the event deletes its jobs (`event_deleted`; INFO `Cron job deleted with its calendar event` per job; RPC `calendar.delete` reports them as `cron_jobs`). An event change that would let a job that cannot fire run again checks that job's target and selected Session on the Session pool (`check_event_change`) and refuses the whole change with `EventJobTargetMissingError` when one no longer exists ("This event change would let a cron job run again whose target no longer exists: <job> (<problem>). Change each job's target or Session, or delete the job, first."); a target that exists but cannot run is allowed because it can recover. Failure stop, enable/disable and statuses work as for other jobs. `list_jobs(event_id=)` lists an event's jobs, `bound_event` reads a job's event; RPC jobs carry `event_id`, `event_edge`, `event_offset_minutes` and `event_title`, and projected rows `event_id` and `occurrence_id` (`tests/core/automation/test_cron_events.py`).
