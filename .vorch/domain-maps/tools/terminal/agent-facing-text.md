# Terminal Agent-facing text

Failure modes F1-F6: `tools/agent-facing-text.md` -> Failure modes. "Usage analysis", "interface audit" and "harness survey" are the 2026-10-04 studies behind the shell/terminal redesign (`tools/shell/agent-facing-text.md`; Sessions and scenario drivers, counts only). "Eval round 1" is the 2026-10-05 evaluation of this interface (40 scripted runs, findings E2-E4), and "terminal review" the 2026-10-05 review of its terminal side (findings R1-R10, each reproduced with the Tool). "Review finding N" (1-12) and "review T1-T5" are the 2026-10-13 review of input and screen fidelity and an independent review folded into it, each checked with real ConPTY programs through the Tool; "code review R1-R13" and "fidelity review E1-E7" the second round of that day (regressions in that work, and differences from xterm.js), each reproduced through the Tool; "user decision D1-D6" the user decisions recorded in `tools/terminal.md` -> Boundary and invariants, and "user review 2026-10-13" the wording cuts approved with them. "Session review 2026-10-16" is the review of the Tool calls in the Sessions of 2026-10-15/16, with probes through the Tool. Texts show the variant with the shell Tool offered as `powershell` unless noted, and are the same at every Session depth; `<id>`, `<label>` (the name, else the program), `<state>` and `N` are filled with real values, and `<call action>` stands for `{"action": "<action>", "terminal_id": "<id>"}`.
