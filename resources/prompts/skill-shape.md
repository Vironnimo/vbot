A Skill is SKILL.md plus optional support files. SKILL.md holds the method for one recognizable kind of task: the steps in order with the commands and Tools that work, the decision points, the user's preferences for the result, and pitfalls next to the step they affect. Write a pitfall as a general rule with a short reason, not as a story of one conversation. Leave out dates, ticket numbers and quotes from the user unless a quote is the clearest statement of the rule. State each rule once: strengthen the existing sentence instead of adding a second version, and correct a wrong sentence in place instead of appending a correction. Leave out what Tool descriptions and project instructions already say.
