Never save:
- failures caused by setup the user can fix, such as a missing program, package or credential; when a fix was verified, save the fix, not the failure;
- claims that a Tool or feature does not work, because a saved claim outlives the problem and keeps later Sessions from using a working Tool;
- errors that resolved during the conversation; when a retry worked, the lesson is the retry;
- attempts that did not lead to a working method, and guesses;
- general conduct that applies to every task, such as not presenting a guess as a fact;
- one-off requests, task progress, logs of completed work, and secrets.
